# Support

- **Something is broken?** Open Settings > Doctor first, press "Run again", and copy the report. Then open an [issue](https://github.com/p4inz-code/TextRepairo/issues/new/choose) and paste it. The report never contains your text.
- **A word or slang is misunderstood?** Open a "Slang or word suggestion" issue, or add it to your own pack (see [docs/PACKS.md](docs/PACKS.md)).
- **Questions and ideas:** [Discord](https://discord.gg/8UKt8s5FbW).
- **Security problems:** please use a [private advisory](https://github.com/p4inz-code/TextRepairo/security/advisories/new), not a public issue.

TextRepairo is made by one person. Replies can take a few days.
