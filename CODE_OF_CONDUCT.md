# Code of conduct

TextRepairo is a small, friendly project made by one person. Everyone is welcome here, whatever their country, language, level or background.

## What we expect
- Be kind and patient. Many people here are not native English speakers, and that is the whole point of this tool.
- Be respectful when you disagree. Talk about the idea, not the person.
- Stay on topic in issues and discussions. Keep pasted examples free of other people's private information.

## What is not okay
- Harassment, insults, hate speech or personal attacks.
- Sharing someone else's private information.
- Spam, advertising or anything meant to mislead people.

## What happens
Comments that break these rules can be edited or removed, and repeated problems can lead to a ban from the repository. To report something, use the private [security advisory form](https://github.com/p4inz-code/TextRepairo/security/advisories/new) (it also works for conduct reports) or message the maintainer on [Discord](https://discord.gg/8UKt8s5FbW).

This applies to the issue tracker, discussions, pull requests and the project's Discord channels.
